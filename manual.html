INSTRUCTION BOOKLETFOR THE 9F POCKET

BEFORE YOU PLAY

Thank you for picking up BATON QUEST for the 9F POCKET. Please read this booklet before you play, then keep it somewhere safe. A drawer nobody opens is ideal.

A FEW PRECAUTIONS

  • Play in a well-lit room, a sensible distance from the screen.
  • Take a short break every hour. The facilitator insists.
  • Never point the baton at a colleague.
  • The conductor's head is a computer. Please do not type on it.
  • If anything in the game asks you to do everything, forever, say no. Politely.

READING THIS BOOKLET

Turn pages with the arrow keys, a swipe, a click on either page, or a controller. CONTENTS jumps straight to a page. Esc closes the booklet.

BATON QUEST, the 9F POCKET and everyone in this booklet were made up for this game. Any resemblance to your own inbox is a coincidence.

On a computer the game fills the window. MENU, at the top right, opens SYSTEM: the settings (how the picture fills the screen, button sizes, vibration) and your saves. They stay in your browser.

KEEP WHAT YOU EARNSAVING AND SECRETS

SAVING

  • Touch a coffee mug and the game saves.
  • There are three files. Pick one with FILE on the title screen. Each keeps its own levels, tokens, moves and stars.
  • On the web page your saves live in your browser, and updates to the game keep them. To be extra safe: MENU, then SAVES. Save a backup file or copy a code, and restore it on any phone or computer.

SECRETS (A FEW HINTS, NO MORE)

  • The office cat hides once on every floor.
  • Six memos hide on every floor. VEND-9's MEMO FINDER helps.
  • Some walls are not walls. Look for something a little off: a crack, a loose vent, an odd cloud.
  • There are 32 GOLD STARS. Some are for doing things well. Some are for doing things oddly.
  • Finish a level fast enough and you beat its PAR.
  • Every boss you beat teaches you a cheat code. The codex's CODES page keeps every one you know.
  • The show does not end at the credits.

MORE WAYS TO PLAYGAME MODES

BATTLE. Up to four conductors in one arena, last one standing. The next page has the rules.

HIGH SCORE. All 16 stages, three tries and a TOP 5. SPEED RUN. A world or all 16 on one clock, with splits and medals.

SETTING UP A BATTLE

  • PLAYERSEach of four slots: you, a CPU, or nobody, with a colour (ORANGE, TEAL, BERRY, VIOLET) and a name of our own, like TEMPO or FORTE.
  • RULESROUNDS (best of 1, 3 or 5), HEARTS (1 to 5), ITEMS (on or off), CPU (EASY, NORMAL, HARD).
  • ARENAOne from each floor: REPLY ALL, HOT SPARE, SYNC OR SWIM, STAND-UP.

Four controllers can share one screen (A on each joins). Online: HOST ONLINE gives a 6-letter code and a link; friends pick JOIN ONLINE or open the link.

LAST ONE STANDINGBATTLE

  • Everyone has hearts, and only a fall off the arena costs one.
  • Every hit adds WOBBLE, the pips by your name. The more WOBBLE, the further the next hit sends you.
  • A fall resets your WOBBLE. You drop back in blinking, and nobody can hit you while you blink.
  • Last one with hearts wins the round. After 100 seconds it is OVERTIME, and WOBBLE grows twice as fast.

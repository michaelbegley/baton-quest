AUTO: the handheld on a phone or tablet, the whole window with a mouse. SHARP: whole-number pixel steps (crisp, may leave a border). FILL: as big as the window allows. Kept in this browser.

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE
The D-pad, the arrow keys or W A S D, or a controller's stick.
A
Jump. Hold it to jump higher. Space or Z on a keyboard; the bottom button on a controller.
B
Swing the baton. Hold it, then let go, to throw a note. X on a keyboard; the right button on a controller.
C
Dash, once you have QUICK KEYS. C on a keyboard; the left or top button, or a bumper, on a controller.
DOWN
On a thin shelf: drop through it.
UP
Open a door at the end of a level.
START
Pause: the codex, the music, the codes and the map. Enter on a keyboard.

It saves at the coffee mugs, in this browser, in one of three files. On a phone, turn it sideways for a bigger screen, or add it to your home screen to play full screen.
